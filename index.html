Common questions

Before you request a review.

Is this a website redesign?

Not necessarily. The review may show that messaging, lead capture, intake, or follow-up should be fixed before a full rebuild.

Do you replace our current tools?

Only when there is a clear reason. The first goal is to understand the process and make the smallest useful improvement.

Is automation always part of the work?

No. Automation is useful when it removes a repetitive step from a process that already has clear ownership and review.

What can we receive?

Depending on the agreed scope: annotated screenshots, a journey map, a prioritized issue list, and a practical action plan.

How do we start?

Call or email Mission Stack with a short description of your business and the customer-path problem you want reviewed. No scheduling system or web form is connected yet.
