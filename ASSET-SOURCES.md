## Why this stops here

Real photography of this restaurant almost certainly exists and looks promising — 116 photos on the Google listing, several explicitly tagged as food and interior shots, is a good sign, not a "no assets" situation.

But I'm not going to download and embed those photos into a design build, even one marked "private demo," for a reason your brief's own policy anticipates but I can't fully satisfy: those photos are hosted by a third-party aggregator (Restaurant Guru) that re-republishes Google Maps contributor uploads — I have no way to identify who actually holds the rights to each individual photo (the restaurant itself, a diner, or Google), no way to confirm the restaurant has authorized their reuse, and no reliable way to attach correct attribution per photo. Scraping and re-embedding them into any file I generate and publish — even a "private" Artifact, which can still be shared onward — isn't something I can respons­ibly do on a "looks public, so it's probably fine" basis. That's a call the restaurant's own owner or an actual account holder should make, not one I should make unilaterally by pulling files off a review site.

This is a sourcing/rights blocker, not a "the images don't exist" blocker — worth being precise about, since the fix is different.
